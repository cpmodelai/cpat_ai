5  Equation list: CPAT code names

Source: coded/cpat_documentation/equations/Equations_codes.qmd.

Status: first draft. This is the parallel version of Equations.qmd: the same equations, ids and order, with the CPAT variable codes of the codes map (excel_ai/CPAT-AI_CodesMap_v<version>.xlsx) in place of the symbols. Inputs appear under their NameOfParameter (ScenarioDefinition / MTInputs). In the code, the variable of each equation carries the same name.

How to read this list

  • A code is the stem of the full CPAT code <iso>.mit.<variable>.<sector>.<fuel>.<scenario>: here ener with subscripts \(s, f, t\) stands for ener.<sector>.<fuel> in year \(t\). Suffixes as in Excel-AI and the code: _prev is shown as the subscript \(t-1\), the baseline value of the same year as the superscript \(B\) (Excel-AI .ref).
  • Indices and their omission as in the Greek version: every equation holds for each scenario and country, and each year unless shown; “Over …” lists the rest. Sector codes (rod, res, ind, …) and fuel codes (coa, nga, gso, die, lpg, ker, oop, bio, ecy, …) are those of the codes map.
  • A name marked \(\dagger\) is not yet confirmed in the codes map; the appendix lists them.

6 Executive summary: core equations

One year of the model, in the order of the calculation chain (spec section 4.3): policies and prices (computed for all years before the year loop), then fuel use, power, revenues, emissions and GDP, then distribution in its analysis year.

M0 Header: macro series

The macro series of the country and the header rows that read them (Excel-AI sheet Inputs_macro, legacy Mitigation rows 417-467). Quantity-free: computed for all years before the year loop.

M0.1 Macro series past the last data year

\[ \mathtt{gdp\_usd}_t = \begin{cases} \mathtt{ngdp}_t / \mathtt{xr}_t \cdot \mathtt{deflidx}_t & t < \mathtt{BaseYr} \\ \mathtt{gdp\_usd}_{t-1}\,(1 + \mathtt{gdp\_growth}_t) & t \ge \mathtt{BaseYr} \end{cases}, \qquad \mathtt{gdp\_lcu}_t = \mathtt{ngdp\_r}_t \]

Over macro years 2018-2045; series \(x\): US deflator and CPI, real GDP growth, real and nominal GDP, exchange rate, population. Excel-AI notation. Extensions: US deflator linear (\(2x_{t-1} - x_{t-2}\)); US CPI, exchange rate flat; real GDP grown with \(g\); nominal GDP with its last growth rate, held from the last data year of nominal GDP (Excel-AI: the growth of the last data year is the year before’s); population with its last growth factor. The exchange rate of a euro country is the euro area’s (WEO G163). Source: Excel-AI: Inputs_macro (build_inputs_macro).

M0.2 Inflation and deflator indices, real GDP, exchange rate

\[ \mathtt{infl}_t = \mathtt{CPIRes} / \mathtt{pcpi}_t, \qquad \mathtt{defl}_t = \mathtt{DeflRes} / \mathtt{ngdp\_d}_t, \qquad \mathtt{xr}_t = \mathtt{gdp.lcu}_t / \mathtt{gdp.usd}_t \]

\(R\) is the results year (MTInputs ResultsYear, read from the baseline column). \(\nu_t\) converts nominal domestic prices and policy inputs to real, \(\delta_t\) international prices. Excel-AI notation. Source: Excel-AI: Mitigation rows 8-13 (section 0).

M1 Policies

Every policy is a path from a start year to a target year, set per scenario.

M1.1 Policy path (linear from a start to a target level)

\[ \mathtt{path}(t;\ \mathtt{start\_year},\ \mathtt{target\_year},\ \mathtt{start\_level},\ \mathtt{target\_level}) \]

Excel-AI notation (the methodology describes the paths in words). Held flat after \(T_1\) unless the input says the path continues. Source: Excel-AI: Equations.md section 0 (Λ, Λ̄).

M1.2 National carbon tax per tonne

\[ \mathtt{cptraj}_t = \mathtt{path}(t;\ \mathtt{CPIntro},\ \mathtt{CPOutro},\ \mathtt{CPLevelStart},\ \mathtt{CPLevelTarget})\cdot \mathtt{infl}_t \]

\(\nu_t\) is the CPI index (US CPI of the results year over year \(t\)) if the inputs are nominal, else 1 (Excel-AI notation). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Prices > Notation (\(NCT\)); Excel-AI: E1.

M1.3 New carbon tax and ETS cost by sector

\[ \mathtt{ctr}_{s,t} = \mathtt{cptraj}_t\,\mathtt{ctcov}_{s}\,(1 - \mathtt{etsc}_{s,t}), \qquad \mathtt{etr}_{s,t} = \mathtt{ets.pe}_t\,\mathtt{etsc}_{s,t}\,(1 - \mathtt{obr}_{s,t}) \]

Over sectors \(s\) (power and the 16 subsectors). \(\kappa_s\) new-ETS coverage, \(\omega_s\) share allocated free (output-based), \(\tilde{p}^E\) the tax-equivalent ETS price (Excel-AI notation; methodology \(NETSP\) is the national ETS price). Where the new tax complements an existing ETS, \(\varphi_{NCT,s}\) is one minus the existing ETS coverage. Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Prices > Notation (\(NCT\), \(NETSP\), \(\varphi_{NCT/ETS}\)); Excel-AI: E5, E6, E13-E15.

M1.4 Shadow price of non-pricing policies by sector

\[ \mathtt{shpx}_{s,t} = \mathtt{fb}_{g(s),t}\,\mathtt{ssc}_{s} + \mathtt{reg}_{s,t} + \mathtt{ets.pe}_t\,\mathtt{etsc}_{s,t}\,\mathtt{obr}_{s,t}, \qquad \mathtt{reg}_{s,t} = \mathtt{cp.shd}_t\,\mathtt{regadj}\,\mathtt{regc}_{s} \]

Over sectors \(s\); \(g(s)\) is the sector group of \(s\). Excel-AI notation: \(b_g\) feebate rate of the feebate sector, \(a\) share of the feebate on the efficiency margin (SPPEffAdjFeebates where the feebate covers \(s\)). \(reg\): under a main scenario with shadow pricing (MTInputs ScenarioSelected; table of main scenarios), the shadow carbon price (\(\Lambda\) less the explicit price) times the efficiency adjustment of the scenario, in the sectors it covers. The shadow price acts on the efficiency margin only (M3.1); it raises no revenue. Source: Excel-AI: E3, E16.

M1.5 Fuel price reform path

\[ \mathtt{fpr}_{c,t},\quad \mathtt{fprj}_c = \frac{\mathtt{fpr.f}_c - \mathtt{fpr.s}_c}{\mathtt{FprOutro} - \mathtt{FprIntro}},\quad \mathtt{fprk}_c = \left(\frac{\mathtt{fpr.f}_c}{\mathtt{fpr.s}_c}\right)^{1/(T_1 - T_0)} - 1,\quad \mathtt{fprp}_c = \mathtt{fpr.s}_c + \mathtt{fprj}_c\,\max(T_1 - T_0 - 1, 0) \]

Over price fuels \(c\) (coal and gas by power, residential, industry; electricity residential and industry; oil products and biomass). Excel-AI notation. The path after the target year follows ExtendCarbonPriceBeyondOutro: Linear* continues the line, Percentage grows at the rate \(k\) from \(p\), otherwise held at \(x_1\). Where the start is zero the rate divides by \(x_0 + j\). Source: Excel-AI: Mitigation section 1 (fprj, fprk, fprp, fpra, fpr).

M1.6 Social cost of carbon

\[ \mathtt{scc}_{nat,t},\ \mathtt{scc}_{glo,t} \in \{\mathtt{scc}_{tgt,t},\ \mathtt{scc}_{rkn|rkg,t},\ \mathtt{scc}_{man,t},\ \mathtt{scc}_{epa,t}\} \]

Excel-AI notation. Target-consistent price, Ricke et al. (2018) national or global (sheet SCC, by discount rate and elasticity), manual path, US EPA; the national and global SCC are chosen by NSCCSource and GSCCSource (read from the baseline column). Source: Excel-AI: Mitigation section 1 (scc).

M1.7 Phase-out factor of subsidies and price controls

\[ \mathtt{pof}_{k,t};\quad \mathtt{csf}_{c,t} = 1 - \mathtt{csi}_c\,(1 - \mathtt{pof}_{cs,t}),\quad \mathtt{pcf}_{c,t} = 1 - \mathtt{pci}_c\,(1 - \mathtt{pof}_{pc,t}),\quad \mathtt{psf}_{f,t} = 1 - \mathtt{psi}_f\,(1 - \mathtt{pof}_{ps,t}) \]

Over phase-outs \(k\): producer subsidies, consumer subsidies, price controls; price fuels \(c\) or producer fuels \(f\). Excel-AI notation. The baseline uses the baseline switch, period and share; a policy scenario its own switch and period and the larger of the two shares. Price controls are on only where the policy switch is on (and, in the baseline, the baseline switch too); they use the consumer-subsidy shares (Excel-AI issue I48). Source: Excel-AI: Mitigation section 1 (po.on, po.n, po.sh, pof, csf, pcf, psf).

M1.8 Methane fee

\[ \mathtt{mfee}_t = \mathtt{mfee.p}_t\,\mathbb{1}[o > 1] + \mathtt{cptraj}_t\,\mathbb{1}[\mathtt{CTforMethane}] \]

Excel-AI notation. The fee path continues linearly after its target year. Source: Excel-AI: Mitigation section 1 (mfee.p, mfee).

M2 Energy prices

Prices before policies by sector group \(g\) and fuel \(f\) (the methodology’s price sectors), then new policies and the after-tax price by sector \(s\) and fuel \(f\). All quantity-free: computed for all years before the year loop.

M2.1 Supply price (fixed part plus a floating part that follows the international price)

\[ \mathtt{sp}_{t} = \mathtt{spf} + (\mathtt{sp}_{t-1} - \mathtt{spf} - \mathtt{ps}_{t-1})\,\frac{\mathtt{gpi}_t}{\mathtt{gpi}_{t-1}} + \mathtt{ps}_t \]

Over sector groups \(g\), fuels \(f\); projection years \(t > L\) (last historical price year). The methodology writes \(sp = fixsp + fltsp + ps\) with \(fltsp_t = fltsp_{t-1}\,gp_t/gp_{t-1}\). \(gp\) is the international price of the price fuel’s commodity (oil, coal, the country’s gas market; biomass flat), real, times the High / Low factor after \(L\); \(ps\) the producer subsidy per unit (A2.5). Historical years: data (A2.4). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Appendix C > Calculations (supply price, floating portion); Excel-AI: E19, E20.

M2.2 Excise and other taxes, floating with the pass-through

\[ \mathtt{txf}_t = \mathtt{txf}_{t-1} - (\mathtt{sp}_t - \mathtt{sp}_{t-1})\,(1 - \mathtt{pcc}) \]

Over sector groups \(g\), fuels \(f\). \(pcc\) is the price-control (pass-through) coefficient: 1 for a liberalised market, 0 for fixed prices. A positive tax does not fall below its last historical level (appendix A2). Here \(txo\) holds existing taxes and subsidies only; the methodology’s \(txo\) also includes the carbon prices, which Excel-AI keeps apart (M2.5). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Appendix C > Calculations (\(flts\), \(pcc\)); Excel-AI: E21-E23.

M2.3 VAT and retail price before new policies

\[ \mathtt{vat}_t = (\mathtt{sp}_t + \mathtt{txo}_t)\,\mathtt{vr}, \qquad \mathtt{pbc}_t = (\mathtt{sp}_t + \mathtt{txo}_t)(1 + \mathtt{vr}) \]

Over sector groups \(g\), fuels \(f\). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Appendix C > Calculations (VAT, retail price); Excel-AI: E24, E25.

M2.4 New carbon price, ETS cost and excise per unit of energy

\[ \mathtt{ctxnew}_{s,f} = \mathtt{ctr}_s\,\mathtt{ef}_{s,f}\,\mathtt{ctcov}_f\,\mathtt{exf}_{s,f}, \quad \mathtt{ets}_{s,f} = \mathtt{etr}_s\,\mathtt{ef}_{s,f}\,\mathtt{exf}_{s,f}, \quad \mathtt{ntx}_{s,f} = \frac{\mathtt{fpr}_{g,f}}{\mathtt{gju}^{\dagger}_{g,f}}\,\mathtt{exf}_{s,f} \]

Over sectors \(s\), fuels \(f\); \(g\) is the price sector of \((s,f)\). \(ef\) the calibrated CO2 emission factor (M7.1), \(x\) the exemption factor (0 for an exempt fuel), \(F^x\) the fuel-price reform path (M1.1) in price units and \(u\) GJ per price unit (Excel-AI notation for \(x\), \(F^x\), \(u\)). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Appendix C > Calculations (\(ncp = nct + netsp\), \(nexc\)); Excel-AI: E26-E29.

M2.5 After-tax price

\[ \mathtt{atp}_{s,f} = \max\!\big(\mathtt{rpb}_{g,f} + (\mathtt{ctxnew}_{s,f} + \mathtt{ets}_{s,f} + \mathtt{ntx}_{s,f} + \mathtt{xcpd}_{s,f})\,(1 + \mathtt{vatr}^{\dagger}_{g,f}),\ \mathtt{PriceFloor}\big) \]

Over sectors \(s\), fuels \(f\). \(\Delta xcp\) is the change in existing carbon pricing against the price data, which already contain it (appendix A2). \(\underline{p}\) is the price floor (0.01). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Appendix C > Calculations (retail price); Excel-AI: E30, E31.

M2.6 Shadow price per unit of energy

\[ \mathtt{shp}_{s,f} = \mathtt{shpx}_{s}\,\mathtt{ef}_{s,f} \]

Over sectors \(s\), fuels \(f\). Excel-AI notation (the methodology names the shadow price in words only). Source: Excel-AI: E32.

M3 Fuel use

The core behavioural equation of CPAT (switchable slot fuel demand, spec section 7).

M3.1 Fuel use, constant-elasticity form

\[ \frac{\mathtt{enel}_{s,f,t}}{\mathtt{enel}_{s,f,t-1}} = \left(\frac{1}{1 + \mathtt{eff\_imp}_{s,f} + \mathtt{addeff}^{\dagger}_{s,t}}\right)^{1 + \mathtt{el\_dem}} \mathtt{shock}_t\,(1 + \mathtt{gdp.pos.pct}_t)^{\mathtt{el\_inc}\cdot\mathtt{incf}_t} \left(\frac{\mathtt{atp}_{s,f,t}}{\mathtt{atp}_{s,f,t-1}}\right)^{\mathtt{el\_dem}} \left(\frac{\mathtt{atp}_{s,f,t} + \mathtt{shp}_{s,f,t}}{\mathtt{atp}_{s,f,t-1} + \mathtt{shp}_{s,f,t-1}}\right)^{\mathtt{el\_eff}\,(1 + \mathtt{el\_dem})} \]

Over sectors \(s\), fuels \(f\); the elasticities \(\epsilon_Y, \epsilon_U, \epsilon_F\) vary by country, sector and fuel. As the methodology, with three Excel-AI additions: the shadow price enters the efficiency term only; \(\alpha^{+}_{s}\) is the additional policy-induced efficiency gain of the sector’s group (Excel-AI notation); \(inc_t\) adjusts the income elasticity to the income level (Excel-AI notation). \(\Psi_t\) is the demand shock (methodology: Covid adjustment; in Excel-AI the 2023-2024 shock, switch on). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Fuel Consumption Dynamics; Excel-AI: E69 (LAMBDAs FUELUSE, AEEIFACTOR).

M3.2 Fuel use used by the rest of the model

\[ \mathtt{ener}_{s,f} = \begin{cases} \mathtt{trfc}^{\dagger}_{f} & s = \mathtt{rod},\ \mathtt{TrModel} = \text{Engineer} \\ \mathtt{enel}_{s,f} + \mathtt{lkc}_{s,f} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \]

Over sectors \(s\), fuels \(f\). \(F^e\) is M3.1, \(F^T\) the road engineer model (M5), \(Lk\) residential leakage to biomass (appendix A3, off by default). Excel-AI notation for the superscripts. Source: Excel-AI: E69, ROADSWITCH, section 6b.

M3.3 Energy use totals and change against the baseline

\[ \mathtt{ener}_{S,all}, \ \mathtt{ener}_{all,f} = \textstyle\sum_s \mathtt{ener}_{s,f} + \mathtt{pe}_{pow,f}, \ \mathtt{ener}_{all,all} = \textstyle\sum_s \mathtt{ener}_{s,all} + \mathtt{pet}, \ \mathtt{echg} = \mathtt{ener} - \mathtt{ener}^{base}, \ \mathtt{epct} = \mathtt{echg} / (\mathtt{ener} - \mathtt{echg}) \]

Over sector groups \(S\) (transport, buildings, industry, other energy use, power), fuels \(f\), years \(t\). Excel-AI notation. \(F^{pow}\) the fuel input of power generation (A4); renewables in aviation and navigation count in the subsector and group totals and in the grand total, not in a fuel total. The baseline value is scenario 1’s of the same year (Excel-AI REFCOL). Electricity demand totals (eldem) sum the subsector demand by group. Source: Excel-AI: Mitigation section 11.

M4 Power

Electricity demand, generation, capacity and prices: the engineer model (standard-CPAT). Generation types \(f\): coal, gas, oil, nuclear, wind, solar, hydro, other renewables, biomass.

M4.1 Electricity demand by sector (last year’s electricity price)

\[ \mathtt{dem}_{s,t} = \mathtt{dem}_{s,t-1}\cdot \mathtt{mult}_{d(s),t}, \qquad \mathtt{pdem}_{d,t} = w_d\,\mathtt{rp}_{res,t-1} + (1 - w_d)\,\mathtt{rp}_{ind,t-1} \]

Over sectors \(s\); \(d = d(s)\) the demand group (transport, residential, industry, services), \(w_d\) its price weight. The methodology’s form, with Excel-AI’s timing: the demand price \(\bar{p}^T\) is a blend of last year’s residential and industrial end-user prices, so power demand does not depend on this year’s power price. The methodology writes the GDP elasticity \(\epsilon_G\) (undefined there); Excel-AI uses \(\epsilon_Y\) with the income factor. Road electricity comes from the road engineer model. Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Engineer model > Power demand; Excel-AI: E52-E54 (LAMBDA DEMANDMULT).

M4.2 Generation required

\[ \mathtt{req}_t = \mathtt{demtot}_t \cdot (1 + \mathtt{net}^{\dagger} + \mathtt{eiu}^{\dagger} + \mathtt{dif}^{\dagger}) \]

Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Engineer model > Power demand; Excel-AI: E57.

M4.3 Variable cost and levelised total investment cost

\[ \mathtt{tvc}_{f,t} = \mathtt{vop}^{\dagger}_{f,t} + \frac{\mathtt{fpp}_{f,t}}{\mathtt{nuu}_f} + \mathtt{ipc}^{\dagger}_{f,t} + \mathtt{ccp}_{f,t}, \qquad \mathtt{tic}_{f,t} = \mathtt{lvc}_{f,t} + \mathtt{lfx}_{f,t} \]

Over generation types \(f\). \(\bar{p}\) is the fuel price averaged over \([t-4, t]\); \(\overline{vc}\) the levelised variable cost, a discounted average of \(vc\) from \(t\) to the last year (computed before the year loop: it depends on prices and policies only). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Power prices > Concepts 1-2; Excel-AI: E37-E43, E46.

M4.4 Dispatch between coal and gas (logit)

\[ \mathtt{lgd}_{f} = \frac{\exp(-\mathtt{K\_dispatch}^{\dagger}\cdot \mathtt{tvc}_f/\min(\mathtt{tvc}))}{\sum_{f'}\exp(-\mathtt{K\_dispatch}^{\dagger}\cdot \mathtt{tvc}_{f'}/\min(\mathtt{tvc}))} \]

Over \(f \in\) {coal, gas}; other types run at their capacity factors. Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Engineer model > Dispatch decision; Excel-AI: E57-E60 (LAMBDAs DISPATCH, LOGITW).

M4.5 Capacity

\[ \mathtt{cap}_{f,t} = \mathtt{cap}_{f,t-1} - \mathtt{ret}_{f,t-1} + \mathtt{nim}_{f,t-1}, \qquad \mathtt{ret}_{f,t} = \mathtt{cap}_{f,t} / \mathtt{lif}^{\dagger}_f \]

Over generation types \(f\). The methodology writes the stock equation with \(g\); it is capacity. Coal retirements follow plant data where available, plus cost-based early retirement (appendix A4). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Engineer model > Retirement and capacity needed; Excel-AI: E56, E61, E62.

M4.6 Investment allocation (logit with scale-up limits)

\[ \mathtt{lgi}_{f,t} = \mathtt{zeta}^{\dagger}_f\,\frac{\exp(-\mathtt{K\_inv}^{\dagger}\cdot\mathtt{tic}_f/\min(\mathtt{tic}))}{\sum_{f'}\exp(-\mathtt{K\_inv}^{\dagger}\cdot\mathtt{tic}_{f'}/\min(\mathtt{tic}))} \]

Over generation types \(f\). A second round allocates what the scale-up limits leave unallocated to the cheapest types (appendix A4). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Engineer model > Investment decision; Excel-AI: E63-E67 (LAMBDAs FIRSTROUND, CHEAPFILL).

M4.7 End-user electricity price

\[ \mathtt{sc}_{g,t} = \mathtt{gncav}_{t-1} + \mathtt{tmc}^{\dagger}_g, \quad \mathtt{rppre}_{g,t} = \mathtt{rppre}_{g,t-1} + \mathtt{rp.ctrl}_g\,(\mathtt{sc}_{g,t} - \mathtt{sc}_{g,t-1})(1 + \mathtt{vatr}^{\dagger}_g), \quad \mathtt{rp}_{g,t} = \mathtt{rppre}_{g,t} + (\mathtt{ccpav}_t\,\mathtt{rp.ctrl}_g\,(1 - \mathtt{rbs}) + \mathtt{pex}_{g,t})(1 + \mathtt{vatr}^{\dagger}_g) \]

Over user groups \(g\) (residential, industry); \(gnc^{av} = \sum_f gns_f\,gnc_f\) the generation-weighted generation cost. Excel-AI’s form (data up to the last historical year, then cost changes passed through at \(pcc_g\); the supply cost uses last year’s generation cost). The methodology instead adds a markup \(mu^T\) calibrated to historical retail prices; Excel-AI’s subsidy gap \(p^0/(1+vatrate) - sp^T\) plays that role (appendix A4). \(reb\) is the rebate switch, \(pex\) the power excise. Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Power prices > Concept 4; Excel-AI: E48-E51.

M5 Road transport (engineer model)

Excel-AI notation throughout (the methodology’s road chapter covers externalities, not the engineer model). Vehicle classes: passenger, freight, 2-3 wheelers; technologies: ICE, BEV, PHEV.

M5.1 Vehicle ownership (Gompertz target, partial adjustment)

\[ \mathtt{trown}^{\dagger}_t = \mathtt{TrOwnAdj}^{\dagger}\cdot \mathtt{trownt}^{\dagger}_t + (1 - \mathtt{TrOwnAdj}^{\dagger})\cdot \mathtt{trown}^{\dagger}_{t-1} \]

Over vehicle classes; growth limited per year. \(y_t\) GDP per capita, \(Sat\) saturation level, \(J\) calibration to observed ownership. The only partial-adjustment equation in Excel-AI. Source: Excel-AI: Methods Transport, T1-T2 (LAMBDAs OWNDEM, GOMPTGT).

M5.2 Electric-vehicle share of purchases (logit on total cost of ownership)

\[ \mathtt{trev}^{\dagger}_t = \frac{\exp(-k\cdot\mathtt{tco}^{\dagger}_{ev,t}/\overline{\mathtt{tco}}_t)}{\sum_v \exp(-k\cdot \mathtt{tco}^{\dagger}_{v,t}/\overline{\mathtt{tco}}_t)} \]

Over vehicle classes. Source: Excel-AI: Methods Transport (demand and choice).

M5.3 Road fuel use

\[ \mathtt{trfc}^{\dagger}_{f,t} = \sum_{v} \mathtt{trkm}_{v,f,t}\cdot \mathtt{trafg}_{v,f,t} \]

Over road fuels \(f\); vehicle classes \(v\). \(VKT\) vehicle-km, \(FE\) fuel use per km of the stock (Excel-AI notation). Source: Excel-AI: Methods Transport T16 (LAMBDA DEMROAD).

M6 Revenues

Revenue in USD million: fuel use in ktoe times \(\theta\) (PJ per ktoe) times a price component in USD/GJ.

M6.1 Revenue from new policies

\[ \mathtt{rnew}_{s,f} = \theta\,\mathtt{ener}_{s,f}\,\big(\mathtt{ctxnew}_{s,f} + \mathtt{ntx}_{s,f} + \mathtt{ets.rf}\cdot\mathtt{ets}_{s,f} + \mathtt{vatr}^{\dagger}\,(\mathtt{ctxnew}_{s,f} + \mathtt{ets}_{s,f} + \mathtt{ntx}_{s,f})\big) \]

Over sectors \(s\), fuels \(f\) (power adds its own terms, appendix A6). \(\rho\) the ETS revenue factor (auctioned part; Excel-AI notation). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Fiscal revenues > Overview (\(rev = F\,\varphi\,ncp\)); Excel-AI: E73.

M6.2 Revenue from existing taxes and cost of existing subsidies

\[ \mathtt{rtx}_{s,f} = \theta\,\mathtt{ener}_{s,f}\,(\mathtt{etx} + \mathtt{vat} + \mathtt{xcpd}_{s,f}\,(1 + \mathtt{vatr}^{\dagger})), \qquad \mathtt{rsub}_{s,f} = \theta\,\mathtt{ener}_{s,f}\,\mathtt{esub} \]

Over sectors \(s\), fuels \(f\). \(txo^{+}\) and \(txo^{-}\) are the positive (taxes) and negative (subsidies, negative) parts. Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Fiscal revenues > Total revenues raised by policy; Excel-AI: E71, E72.

M6.3 Total revenue

\[ \mathtt{rtot} = \mathtt{rnet} + \mathtt{rnew} + \mathtt{rmf}, \qquad \mathtt{rnet} = \mathtt{rtx} + \mathtt{rsub} \]

\(rev^{pow}\) power-sector revenue (appendix A6), \(rev^{mf}\) methane fee (appendix A7). Source: Excel-AI: E75, E76.

M7 Emissions

M7.1 Energy-related CO2

\[ \mathtt{co2}_{s,f} = \theta\,\mathtt{ener}_{s,f}\,\mathtt{ef}_{s,f}, \qquad \mathtt{co2.tot} = \sum_{s,f}\mathtt{co2}_{s,f} \]

Over sectors \(s\) (including power: generation fuel use), fuels \(f\). \(ef\) carries the calibration to the inventory (appendix A7; off by default in Excel-AI). Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Emissions > Energy-related emissions; Excel-AI: E77, E78.

M7.2 Other greenhouse gases (pattern)

\[ \mathtt{ghg}_{u,p,t} = \mathtt{ghg}_{u,p,t-1}\cdot \mathtt{ghg.dr}_{u,t} \]

Over UNFCCC sectors \(u\), gases \(p\). Each source grows with its driver (energy CO2, industrial energy CO2, GDP and population with elasticities); the source equations are in appendix A7. Source: methodology: 03_Mitigation, Emissions (N2O, IPPU, agriculture, LULUCF, waste); Excel-AI: E80-E86.

M8 GDP

Excel-AI notation throughout: the methodology describes the multipliers but gives no in-model equation.

M8.1 Fiscal space and impulse

\[ \mathtt{fis.rev} = \mathtt{rnet} + \mathtt{rnew} + \mathtt{rsr} + \mathtt{rpsu} + \mathtt{ch4.rev}, \quad \mathtt{fis.sp}_t = \frac{\mathtt{fis.rev}_t - \mathtt{fis.ref}_t}{\mathtt{gdp.usd}_t \cdot 1000}, \quad \mathtt{fis.imp}_t = \mathtt{fis.sp}_t - \mathtt{fis.sp}_{t-1}\frac{\mathtt{gdp.usd}_{t-1}}{\mathtt{gdp.usd}_t} \]

Excel-AI notation: the revenue is the whole fiscal balance of the energy taxes and subsidies (existing, new, price-control wedges, producer subsidies, methane fee), against the baseline (\(\mathtt{fis.ref}\)); the impulse starts in GDPStartYear (in the base year it is the whole fiscal space). \(\mathtt{fis.impp}\) is the impulse from the carbon-pricing start year (CPIntro), used for the recycled revenue. \(\mathtt{fis.lag}_{l}\), \(\mathtt{fis.lagp}_{l}\) hold the impulses of the 19 earlier years.
