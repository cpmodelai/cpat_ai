CPAT and the CPAT-AI project

The Climate Policy Assessment Tool (CPAT), developed by the World Bank and the IMF, gives a rapid assessment of climate mitigation policies for more than 180 countries: their effects on energy prices and use, emissions, revenue, GDP, households, air pollution and transport.

CPAT is a large spreadsheet model, hard to read, slow to open and hard to change. The CPAT-AI project rebuilds its engine with AI coding agents, under written norms, in two forms that share one set of equations, inputs, codes and data: readable Excel workbooks (Excel-AI) and a Python model (CPAT Python). Every number is checked by independent recomputation, and every difference from legacy CPAT is either a fixed bug or a tracked issue.

The three parts of this site

Methodology

The CPAT documentation: the methods of the mitigation, multipliers, distribution, air-pollution and road-transport modules, with the corrections the project has made to it.

CPAT Excel-AI

The Excel prototypes: the mitigation prototype and its user guide, the method of every block, the distribution and air-pollution modules, the legacy bugs and issues, the data.

CPAT Python

Coded CPAT, the Python model: how to run, edit and test it, how it works, and every equation, table, input and variable.
