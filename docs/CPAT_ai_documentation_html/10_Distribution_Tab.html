10  The distribution tab

Source: excel_ai/mitigation/docs/methods/Distribution.md.

The household incidence of a scenario in one analysis year, by decile. It follows legacy CPAT’s sheet Distribution and its LiveOutputs rows 911-1033, with legacy’s bugs fixed.

  • Builder: build.py (build_distribution, build_distribution_data, build_mto_distribution).
  • Data: extract_distn.py writes data/distn_*.csv.
  • Independent check: distribution_ref.py, a Python reference.
  • Legacy formulas: condensed in ../distribution/ (see the plan, PLAN_Distribution_in_MVP.md).

1. Layout (NORMS 1.1b)

Part Content
Rows 1-12 Row 1 holds 0 (shown as Basket) and the deciles 1-10. Rows 4-5: scenario name and number, formatted as Mitigation (frozen with rows 1-3). Row 6: position of the group. Rows 7-8: Mitigation column of the analysis year in group 1 and in this group. Row 9: statistic. Rows 10-11: survey code parts. Row 12: analysis year, the summary of section 0 (v0.4.8; row 10 before).
Roll-up (v0.4.8, NORMS 2.5) One row group per section: the band and, below the hidden rows, the summary row (last in its section) and one blank row stay visible. Summaries, all overall: 0 analysis year, 1 options check, 2 revenue, 3 average indirect price change of the consumption categories (budget-share weighted), 4 average price factor of the household fuels (budget-share weighted), 5 energy budget share (fuels and biomass), 6 total effect, 7 total consumption, 8 recycling gain, 9 net effect after recycling. Sections 6-9 list urban and rural first, overall last. Columns C:G and I:K are one column group each, rolled up, as Mitigation; zoom 75.
Columns A variable, B item, C sample (ove, urb, rur), D-E parameters (data variable, sample label), H description, I unit, J source (legacy rows). Per group: code column, basket, deciles 1-10, spacer (13 columns, from K; v0.4.8: deciles 9 wide; totals in local currency shown in bn and population in m, values unchanged).
Groups 1 = scenario 1 (levels; effects 0), 2 = the policy scenario, 3 = the LAMBDA test group (runs group 2’s scenario and reads Mitigation’s LAMBDA group). Pasting group 2 to the right adds scenarios 3-6 (NORMS 3e, scenario capacity).
Row kinds Settings and one-value rows: the value in the basket column, repeated across the deciles (so every cell refers to its own column). Decile rows: one formula across basket and deciles. Decile-only rows (recycling): the basket column is blank.
Codes iso.distn.<variable>[.<item>][.dec.<sample>].<scenario>. LiveOutputs reads the legacy codes without the scenario suffix, from the group of the scenario in its column E.

2. Chain (sections of the sheet)

Section Formula Legacy
1 Settings The 23 ScenarioDefinition distribution settings of the group’s scenario (rows 197-205, 304-319). Derived: infrastructure-access variable, ASPIRE program, most-used cooking fuel. A support check names any option v0.4.4 does not model. B.I
2 Inputs Household price change by fuel: residential after-tax price atp.<f>.res (electricity: retail price rp.ecy.res) / scenario 1 - 1. Economy-wide change: rpn (coal and gas: industry; oil products: all sectors; electricity: rp.ecy.ind). Scenario-1 base-year spending: pav.sp. Macro inputs: GDP (LCU), population, new-policy revenue (rnew x xr). B.II-B.III, C.I 528-556, I152-I237
3 Indirect prices Category price change = Σ_f Lcat(c,f) x g_f. Lcat is the household-demand weighted Leontief coefficient (IO_GTAP table). g_f = R_f / T_f, with R_f = Σ spending x price change of the fuel group and T_f = total embedded plus own use. C.I 496-736
4 Price factors dp x (1 + dp)^ε (HH_Elast, decile-specific; PEDs). The exempt cooking fuel is 0 for the bottom deciles. C.II 801-825
5 Budget shares HHSurvey by sample, statistic (row 9) and decile. C.III-C.IV
6 Effects Effect = -price factor x budget share x loss scale (% of consumption, loss negative). Loss scale = behavioural factor (InferredDist: revenue / implied revenue) + DWLTri x (1 - factor) if DWLs. C.V-C.VII, D.II
7 Rebasing Population = survey population x population / survey population. Per-capita consumption = survey value x national consumption / (survey basket x population). National consumption = GDP x consumption share (WDI, latest year). C.VIII
8 Recycling PIT (personal allowance, proportional, targeted exemption). Targeted cash transfer (bottom percentile, coverage, leakage). Public investment (people without access). Current spending (ASPIRE quintiles). All per capita, then in % of each sample’s consumption. C.X-C.XI
9 Net, compensation, Gini Net effect = effect + recycling. Compensation share = loss x consumption / Σ. Gini = 1 - SUMPRODUCT(consumption, DGiniW) / SUM(consumption), base, after the price effects and after recycling. C.IX, C.XII, D.V

Two identities let the sheet stay short:

  1. Indirect prices (ScaleGTAP = Yes, legacy default).
    • Legacy rescales each GTAP sector’s raw price change by k(s) = Σ_f σ(f,s) R_f / Σ_f Emb(f,s) dp(f,s).
    • With Emb(f,s) = Y_s L(f,s) and σ(f,s) = Emb(f,s) / T_f, the sector’s adjusted change is Σ_f L(f,s) R_f / T_f. The sector-specific fuel prices cancel.
    • Checked on legacy’s cache: all 59 sectors to 4e-16, and the 14 categories to 3e-6 (legacy prints 6 digits).
    • ScaleGTAP = No uses g_f = R_f / spending_f, i.e. the economy-wide price change. Legacy instead uses the price change of each GTAP sector’s CPAT sector; that simplification is recorded.
  2. Gini.
    • Legacy fits L(x) = a x² + b x to the cumulative decile shares (LINEST, no intercept) and takes Gini = (5 - area) / 5.
    • The fit is linear in L, so Gini = 1 - Σ_j s_j W_j with fixed weights W_j (DistMapping, name DGiniW; distribution_ref.gini_weights).
    • Replicated as an issue: the fitted curve, not the trapezoid rule.

3. Bugs fixed (not replicated; NORMS 0)

Legacy Bug v0.4.4
R2562:Z2562 The targeted share of a decile is capped at 10 points, not its population share: 105.6% of decile 2 receives the transfer. Capped at the decile’s population share.
C.V/C.VI basket (e.g. Q1607) The basket uses decile 1’s elasticity adjustment and exemption test. The basket uses the basket elasticity and is never exempt.
Q2633 Current spending tests the ASPIRE flag of the targeted transfer (I213), not of current spending (I214). No flag: the ASPIRE program of the chosen type, 0 if absent.
2701-2707 Public investment and current spending are 0 when the transfer coverage or percentile is 0. Independent of the transfer settings.
Q2749 The targeted exemption is capped by all carbon-pricing revenue (I154), not the PIT share (I163); urban and rural values are not per capita. Capped by the PIT revenue; per capita in every sample.
Q3259, Q3263 The urban and rural compensation shares divide by overall consumption weights (they sum to 52 and 47). Each sample’s own weights: each sums to 100.
859-883 Deadweight loss is a price fraction subtracted from a % of consumption. DWLTri x (1 - factor) x the effect (same units).
LiveOutputs 1001-1002 dist.year and dist.stat read ScenarioDefinition column D (0 in dashboard mode). The analysis year and statistic of the group.

The bug fixes change Egypt’s legacy numbers only a little: the targeted transfer in deciles 1-4 moves by up to 0.13 points of consumption, and the Gini change with recycling by 0.0006 points. The urban and rural compensation shares move most, because each now sums to 100.

4. Issues replicated (tracked)

  • Spending year: fuel spending in the base year (legacy: first calculation year), with analysis-year price changes.
  • Deflator: the GTAP values are converted with the local GDP deflator (legacy I244).
  • Elasticities: always overall, mean (the data have no other).
  • Recycled amounts: per capita from the overall sample, applied to urban and rural consumption.
  • Lorenz-fit Gini (section 2).
  • Revenue: revenue from new policies (rnew) stands for legacy’s carbon-pricing revenue (carbon tax, VAT on it, auctioned ETS).

5. Options and outputs added in v0.6.0 (every legacy option now modelled)

  • Targeted transfer (section 8): one formula for the three legacy modes. Weight per decile w = 1 (Cash), the share below the poverty line (TargetBelow, with Cash only; HHSurvey pct_belowpl_* x 10, at most 100%, mean as legacy) or the ASPIRE benefit of the TransferType program for the decile’s quintile. Transfer per capita = revenue x w x share receiving / Σ w x share receiving x population, so the payout equals the revenue (legacy pays 125-129% on the ASPIRE path, L147). A program without data for the country falls back to cash and the settings check says so (legacy drops the transfer, L148). Median statistic: the transfer reaches only deciles where more than half receive (legacy row 2685; the money not paid is not redistributed, as legacy); public investment and current spending are not flagged (L150). Switch tests (Egypt): Cash Transfers program 1, 1, 0.691, 0.691, 0.047 of decile 1; poverty line 6.85: 1, 1, 1, 0.507, 0.003; as the legacy formulas with the fixes applied to legacy’s cached inputs.
  • Outputs for charts (section 10, legacy D.I-D.V): decile labels (QuantilesLCU: consumption brackets in real local currency of the analysis year, L157-L158), budget shares by group, urban and rural direct and indirect effects by item, per-capita (local currency) budgets, effects and net effect, Lorenz curves (baseline as legacy: 4.016, 9.045, 16.106), cumulative compensation, the 25th and 75th percentile households (L155-L156), Gini change in points.
  • Production sectors (section 11, legacy D.VI): input-cost price change of each GTAP sector (Leontief coefficients x fuel-group factors, the identity of section 2), output price after pass-through (PassthroughDist; coefficients by CPAT sector, L163), rank and top 20 (ties broken, L162), 14 CPAT sectors (chemicals and power added, L161) with demand and output shares and weighted prices; LiveOutputs ppic, ppici, outpriin by sector code (L164). Data: data/distn_gtapmap.csv (legacy Mapping F/R/H), data/distn_passthrough.csv (legacy Distribution I470:I486).
  • Charts (sheet Charts, below the mitigation charts): the legacy Dashboard distribution charts from group 2.

6. Checks

  • Python reference: fed with legacy’s cached Egypt inputs, it reproduces legacy’s outputs to the precision of the cache (python distribution_ref.py): effects, recycling, net effect and compensation to 4e-4, Gini changes to 7e-6.
  • Quick check:
    • group 2 of the sheet equals the reference fed with the sheet’s own inputs;
    • the LAMBDA group equals group 2;
    • no error values;
    • scenario numbers align with Mitigation group by group.
  • Paste test (check_paste.py): Distribution groups 4-7 are pasted with Mitigation’s.
