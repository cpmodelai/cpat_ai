## The two modes (ScenarioDefinition, below the last legacy row: `TcbMode`, default `Legacy`)

- **`Legacy` (legacy-consistent):** exactly what legacy 465 does. Vehicle-km, freight vehicle-km, average fuel economy by fuel and the passenger/freight splits come from the **engineer** road model (section 5b) whatever `TrModel` is, while the road share of each fuel follows the **switched** model (`TrModel`; Egypt default `Elasticity`). **This is internally inconsistent** (register issue I100, plan section 1.3 and issue I-a): under `TrModel = Elasticity`, the welfare change follows engineer vehicle-km (Egypt 2031: -8.6%) while the fuel use modelled falls by -12.9% (road gasoline) and -14.3% (diesel). Phase 0 re-checks this on legacy 465. Replicate it, and show a warning on Cover when `TrModel` is not `Engineer`.
- **`Engineer` (connected to the transport engineer model):** every quantity from the engineer road model, consistently: vehicle-km, freight vehicle-km, fuel economy, class splits, **and** the road fuel use and road shares of the engineer model's own fuel use (`trfcp`, `trfcf`, `trfcc`, `trfce` of section 5b) instead of the switched model, so the $/l values and the welfare match the engineer fuel use. Cover states whether Mitigation ran `TrModel = Engineer` (otherwise Mitigation's fuel use and emissions follow the elasticity model and the two do not match). Register this mode as an upgrade (`U`) linked to I100; it is not the default.

Both modes run in the same rows; the switch picks the inputs in one block of input rows (no duplicated calculation blocks).
